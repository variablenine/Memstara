        A NEW 16-MEMBER RING WORKS LIKE

        —

        A black marble flood is one sender making a great many outputs so that other people's wallets pick theirs as decoys, and every decoy they own is one they can rule out. This counts the outputs in each block and works out how many of the decoys a wallet would pick right now come from outputs above a usual day, weighted the way wallets pick (mostly from the last few days). Nobody can see who made those outputs, so a busy spell of ordinary use reads the same.
